# Interface direction

Three routes were considered:

1. **Control room.** Dark canvas, neon status lights, dense telemetry, and animated charts. This fits a live sensor product but would imply data that LabPulse does not collect.
2. **Service desk.** White table, blue actions, compact filters, and a conventional ticket queue. It is familiar, though it gives the physical equipment little presence.
3. **Field ledger.** Warm paper surfaces, dark ink, compact asset codes, and a bench status strip above the incident queue. It makes rooms and equipment feel concrete without suggesting live telemetry.

Field ledger is the direction. The bench strip is the signature: each equipment tile shows its name, room, and current state, and opens the relevant incident list. The layout gives up the density of a full service desk and the drama of a control room. That is acceptable for a class-sized lab inventory.
